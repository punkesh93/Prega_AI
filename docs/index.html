Privacy policy

Your data, in plain words

Everything you log — your profile, daily entries, kick counts, moods, appointments, and journal photos — stays only on this device. There is no account and no server holding your data.

What we store

All app data lives in Prega AI's private storage on your phone, using Android's standard local database. It is never uploaded, backed up to a server, or sold to anyone.

What leaves your phone

Only your messages to the AI coach (plus basic context like your current week) are sent to our AI provider to generate a reply. They are not used to identify you, are not stored against your name, and are never sold. Journal photos and personal logs are never sent anywhere.

We also collect anonymous, aggregate usage counts — things like how many times a day the app is opened, or how many people use a given feature — so we know if something is broken or unused. These counts are never your journal entries, notes, mood, weight, symptoms, or anything else you log; only an event name, the app version, and a random ID generated once on your device that isn't tied to your name, email, or Google account.

Sharing

Nothing is ever shared unless you deliberately tap a share button yourself — for example, sharing a journal memory or your garden to a messaging app of your choice.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details.

Permissions

Photos: accessed only through Android's system photo picker — the app never gets broad gallery access. Steps: read from your phone's built-in step sensor, used only to show your own daily count. Notifications: used only for the app's own reminders.

Deleting your data

"Delete everything" in Settings removes all of it, immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Not medical care

Prega AI offers general information and companionship. It never diagnoses, prescribes, or replaces your midwife or doctor — and it will always tell you when something needs their attention instead of an app's.
